# Final Swatch and Documentation Page — Due 12/10

Turn in one copy of your final swatch for inclusion in the course swatch book alongside a PDF documenting the swatch.

Turn the swatch in, in person, in class on 12/13 or before. Please clearly label the swatch with your name (e.g. put it in a bag with your name on it or add a piece of tape with your name)

Submit a PDF of your documentation page for the course swatch book. Use the template provided in Canvas. This page must include a list of the materials and tools used to create your swatch with enough detail that a different student in the class could implement what you did. Include details about the loom you used, the materials on the warp and the warp density. Depending on the function of your swatch, you can either include a technical circuit diagram or instructions for replicating your swatch. You must also include an illustrated diagram. The illustrated diagram should label all the parts of the swatch, how they function, and how it can be connected to. You must also cite at minimum two sources/references that you used to guide your process.
