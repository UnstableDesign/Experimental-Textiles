# Sample Round 1 Report — Due 11/10

This deliverable will require you to turn in via canvas a weekly-style reflection of your round 1 sampling activities. Use the weekly template to describe the process. Your documentation should include photos of __at least two new swatches__. Identify at least three key decision points in your process where you changed plans, tried something new, or developed a new approach to optimize your swatch towards your heuristic. Describe these key points in writing. This is the primary place in the assignment where I will grade you on your decisions so provide strong reasoning for why you tried different techniques at different times. Include a reflection at the end of the documentation about what you would still like to try or what you think you will attempt in round 2. Turn this in as a URL via canvas.
