# Sample Round 2 Report — Due 11/19

This deliverable will require you to turn in via canvas a weekly-style reflection of your round 2 sampling activities. Use the weekly template to describe the process. Your documentation should include photos of __at least two new swatches__. Identify at least three key decision points in your process where you changed plans, tried something new, or developed a new approach to optimize your swatch towards your heuristic. Describe these key points in writing. This is the primary place in the assignment where I will grade you on your decisions so provide strong reasoning for why you tried different techniques at different times. Include a reflection at the end of the documentation about which swatch you consider to be the furthest along and that you will duplicate for the final. Turn this in as a URL via canvas.
